A distinctive brand identity celebrating the bond between horses, nature, and Moroccan culture through strength, authenticity, and elegance.

Overview

A brand identity inspired by the freedom and nobility of horses.

ALJAWAD is a unique Moroccan project that brings together a passion for horses and nature with culture, education, and leisure. Designed for horse enthusiasts, families, and tourists, it offers an experience where people can discover and appreciate horses in a modern and welcoming environment.

Problem

The challenge was to create a distinctive visual identity that communicates the strength and freedom of horses while reflecting the authenticity of Moroccan culture. The brand needed to balance elegance with simplicity and create a memorable visual presence that connects naturally with its audience.

Solution

The visual identity combines a stylized horse symbol with a distinctive wordmark inspired by Arabic character and elegance. A warm brown palette (#B07E4A), supported by black (#000000) and soft beige (#F1E9DC), reinforces the connection with nature and authenticity. The selected Aller typeface brings clarity, readability, and a modern feel to the brand's communication, creating a consistent identity across different media.
