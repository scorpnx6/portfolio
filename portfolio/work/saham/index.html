Overview

Choose Who You Become. Define Your Identity.

THOR is a brand identity built around a powerful and memorable name, supported by the statement "Choose Who You Become." The concept communicates individuality, self-expression, and the freedom to define one's own identity.

Problem

Creating a recognizable brand identity requires more than a name. It needs a clear message that communicates the brand's personality and leaves a lasting impression. The THOR presentation does not specify a particular design challenge, but its central message focuses on individuality and personal identity.

Solution

The identity presents THOR through a distinctive brand name paired with the slogan "Choose Who You Become." This combination creates a straightforward brand message centered on personal choice, individuality, and transformation. The slogan reinforces the identity by giving the name a clear and memorable meaning.
